### T47 — a play known by touch comes before a LOW clue

- **Position:** stacks r3 y3 g4 b4 p2 d2, 2 tokens, 11 cards left, so pace is 2.
  - will-bot67's o27 (slot 3), clued yellow at T40, reads `{y4}` exactly and is
    playable. It is a play known by touch, never stamped as a call.
  - T46: yagami's Purple to will-bot69 called its o43, the p3.
- **Bot:** will-bot67 gave a 2 to yagami (its Cathy), touching only her trash g2.
  - It was an ordinary reactive. will-bot69's p3 (T48) was already called, so the clue's
    only new play was yagami's r4 (T49).
  - `reactor0.choose_clue`, rung `1.reactive_play`, tier 0 (LOW).
- **Cause:**
  1. *Occupied* (`requires_high_tier`, `src/conventions/reactor0/state_eval.cpp`)
     counted only STAMPED calls. A play known by touch did not make will-bot67
     occupied.
  2. An unoccupied seat is held to the MEDIUM bar only while `pace() >= 3 &&
     clue_tokens <= 3` (`clue_is_admissible`, phase 1 rule 2a). At pace 2 every clue
     was admissible, LOW included.
  3. Phase 1 (clue) runs before phase 2 (play), so the LOW clue won over the y4.
- **Resolution (v23.31.0, the user's ruling; reactor0 and TIIAH alike):**
  - A play Alice knows by touch makes her occupied: a card no clue stamped, every
    reading of which plays on her own view (`requires_high_tier`,
    `src/conventions/reactor0/state_eval.cpp:372-382`, via `Player::obvious_playables`).
  - So the HIGH bar holds at any pace from 1, and she plays rather than give a LOW or
    MEDIUM clue.
  - `build/replay_log.exe logs/will-bot67-2026856.log --turn 47 --rerun`: before
    `clue rank value=2 → player 0` (`1.reactive_play`, tier 0); after
    `play(order=27)`, with the gate's `occupied: true`.
  - Tests: `tests/test_tiiah/test_replay_2026856_known_play_over_low_clue.cpp`,
    `tests/test_reactor0/test_decision_making/test_known_play_occupies.cpp`. Also
    `test_pace_clue_gate.cpp`'s `ObviousPlayableWithoutStampRequiresHigh`, changed with
    the user's approval: until v23.31.0 it asserted the opposite.
  - A/B, TIIAH & Black, seeds 1-200: 26.030 → 25.985 mean, strikeouts 5 → 7. Every
    mover diverged where the old build clued over a touch-known play; the strikeouts
    came 18-24 turns later.
- **Status:** fixed in v23.31.0.
