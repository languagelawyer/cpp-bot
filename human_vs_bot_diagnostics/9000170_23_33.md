## Items

### T58 — one card left, and the seat after next needs two turns: stall

- **Position:** sim-alice to act, 2 tokens, 1 card left. Every stack is on 5 except
  Black, on 2.
  - **sim-cathy:** the clued k3 (o34) and the unclued k5 (o53).
  - **sim-bob:** the clued k4 (o46), read `{r4,g4,b4,k4}`. His only other good-looking
    card, the clued o21, is the b4 and trash.
- **The line that wins:**
  1. sim-alice stalls (T58).
  2. sim-bob stalls (T59).
  3. sim-cathy plays the k3 and draws the last card (T60).
  4. In the final round sim-bob plays the k4 (T62) and sim-cathy the k5 (T63).
  - Any card drawn at T58 or T59 starts the final round too early, and the k5 never
    plays.
  - v23.33.0 played exactly this line and scored 30: Black to sim-bob, then R5 to
    sim-cathy.
- **Bot:**
  - **The search:** `forced_endgame_action` declined. The endgame clue list's rung
    `3.singles_out_useful` then picked Black to sim-bob.
  - **The guard:** `tiiah.no_idle_stall` replaced it with a chuck of o50, since sim-bob
    held no play and the critical k4.
  - **The result:** the game ended 28.
- **Cause:** no forced-endgame rule covers this shape.
  - **Rule 5** needs the NEXT seat to hold two critical cards that are playable at once.
  - **Rule 6** has the same schedule, but at 2 cards left with no token.
  - The guard counts a stall as forced only when a forced-endgame rule fires.
- **Resolution (v23.35.0):** forced-endgame **Rule 7** (`stall_for_two_turns_one_card`;
  tiiah/CONVENTION.md §2y, reactor0 DECISION_MAKING.md step 0). With one card left and
  two tokens, when drawing now gives the seat after next one turn and two stalls give her
  two, CP stalls.
  - **The stall** is the fill-in (`reactor0::choose_fill_in_stall`): a stable clue to Bob
    that gives him nothing to throw, with the smallest good-card product. Black names
    sim-bob's k4, product 1.
  - The stall is forced, so the no-idle-stall guard keeps it.
  - **Rerun:** `build/replay_log.exe logs/diag/noidle_exp_23_33/noidle_exp/sim-alice-9000170.log
    --turn 58 --rerun`: the candidate `discard(order=50)`; v23.35.0 `clue colour value=5 →
    player 1`.
