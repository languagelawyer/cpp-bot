## Items

### T22 — a reactive that re-gets a dark card the pending reaction already gets

- **Position:** stacks r3 y1 g1 b1 p1 d1, 1 token. Every stack view agrees
  (`scripts/tiiah_stacks.py 2027922 22`: `311111` on all seven).
  - At T21, will-bot69's Yellow to yagami is a reactive with will-bot67 reacting.
    will-bot67's o24 is called `[f] r4`.
  - The receiver target of that reaction is yagami's o25, the d2 in slot 1. When
    will-bot67 plays o24, yagami is called to play the d2.
- **Bot:** will-bot67 gave Blue to will-bot69 instead of reacting
  (`reactor0.choose_very_high_clue`, rung `1.reactive_play`). The Blue is a reactive
  with yagami reacting on o25, the same d2, and will-bot69 receiving on o16, the b2.
  It spent the team's last token.
- **Cause:** a reactive play that gets a dark special-suit or Null card played is
  VERY HIGH (v23.14.0), and VERY HIGH outranks a pending reaction.
  - The one exception (v23.21.0, `special_play_already_coming`) needed BOTH of two
    things: a card on the clue that a pending reaction will call anyway, AND a
    receiver card that is already called or known to play.
  - The b2 was new, so the exception did not apply, and the d2 the reaction was
    already getting lifted the Blue to VERY HIGH.
- **Resolution (v23.38.0, the user's ruling; reactor0 `DECISION_MAKING.md`, Clue Tier
  Definitions, and tiiah `CONVENTION.md` §2b):**
  - A reactive is not VERY HIGH when every dark special-suit or Null card it gets is
    one a pending reaction will call anyway (`special_play_already_coming`). The
    plain card it also gets, new or not, earns no VERY HIGH of its own. This
    subsumes v23.21.0's exception, which needed that card to be already called.
  - `build/replay_log.exe logs/will-bot67-2027922.log --turn 22 --rerun`: before
    `clue colour value=3 → player 2` (Blue); after `play(order=24)`, the reaction.
  - Tests:
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_replay_2027922_coming_dark_card_not_very_high.cpp`;
    `test_special_play_already_coming.cpp`'s third case flipped (user-approved) and
    a fourth added.
- **Status:** fixed in v23.38.0.

### T27 — the zero-clue marker outlives the token that clears it

- **Position:** stacks r4 y1 g1 b2 p1 d2, 1 token. Every stack view agrees
  (`411212`).
  - will-bot67 holds o28, an unclued r2 (trash) drawn at T25, in slot 1, and o22,
    the unclued critical d4, in slot 2.
  - T22's Blue took the team to 0 tokens. yagami's T26 discard brought one back.
- **Bot:** will-bot69 gave will-bot67 a 4, read as a lock (`reactor0.choose_clue`,
  rung `3.bob_chop`, stable_lock). This protects the d4 as if it were his chop.
- **Cause:** `Game::chop` skips a card drawn after the team ran out of tokens
  (`zcs_turn`). The marker was cleared only by an action taken from a state that
  still had a token, so yagami's discard at 0 tokens left it set. At T27 the
  marker was still 22, o28 was skipped, and will-bot67's chop read as the d4.
- **Resolution (v23.38.0, the user's ruling; reactor `CONVENTION.md` `chop()`, shared
  by every convention):**
  - A player who begins his turn with a token clears the marker for every other
    player, whether or not he acts; his own clears when someone else begins a turn
    with one (`Game::zcs_turn_starts`, `Game::zcs_gates`).
  - A first version cleared it only for the player after him. On the A/B that also
    kept some markers longer than before, and the user ruled it should clear for
    all the others.
  - At T27 will-bot67's chop is o28, and yagami's marker clears too. will-bot69's
    own stays, since nobody else has begun a turn with a token since T22.
  - `apply_snapshot` no longer restores the recorded `zcs_turn`; it recomputes the
    marker from the history.
  - `build/replay_log.exe logs/will-bot69-2027922.log --turn 27 --rerun`: before
    `clue rank value=4 → player 0` (the lock); after `discard(order=23)`, its own
    chop (`12.discard_chop`; no clue clears the tier gate).
  - Tests: `tests/test_basics/test_zcs_marker.cpp`,
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_replay_2027922_zero_clue_marker_clears_at_turn_start.cpp`.
- **Status:** fixed in v23.38.0.
