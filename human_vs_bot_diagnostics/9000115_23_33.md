### T22 — a save of a good card on chop is no idle stall

- **Position:**
  - **sim-alice:** 5 tokens. The stacks are r0 y2 g2 b1 p2 k3.
  - **sim-bob's hand:** p4 (o27), b1 (o25), the clued y4 (o17), y5 (o15) and the clued
    g5 (o8). He has no playable card.
  - **His chop:** o27, the p4, his newest unclued card. His critical card is the y5,
    which is off chop.
- **Bot:**
  - v23.33.0 gave 4 to sim-bob, a stable discard at `reactor0.choose_clue` rung
    `3.bob_chop`. It touches the p4 on his chop.
  - The candidate's guard (`tiiah.no_idle_stall`, shape `stable_discard`) replaced it with
    a discard of o26.
- **Cause:** `saves_a_critical` (`src/conventions/tiiah/idle_stall.cpp`) treats a clue as
  a save only when Bob's chop is a last usable copy, and the p4 is not one. So a clue that
  saves a good chop counted as an idle stall.
- **Resolution (v23.35.0):** `saves_bobs_chop`. A clue that moves Bob's chop, or gives him
  a known safe discard, while that chop is a good card (not trash, a free chuck or a
  same-hand dupe) is a save, and so no idle stall (tiiah/CONVENTION.md §2x).
  - **Rerun:** `build/replay_log.exe logs/diag/noidle_exp_23_33/noidle_exp/sim-alice-9000115.log
    --turn 22 --rerun`: the candidate `discard(order=26)`; v23.35.0 `clue rank value=4 →
    player 1`.
