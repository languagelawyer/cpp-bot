### T9 — the ordinary reactive's target is walked under stack simulation

- **Position:** stacks r1 y1 g0 b1 p0 d0. Every view agrees
  (`scripts/tiiah_stacks.py 2026727 9`: 110100 for all seven).
  - will-bot67, slots 1-5: o18 r3, o9 b2 (known, `{b2}`), o8 d5 (called to discard),
    o6 b3 (clued), o5 y5.
  - yagami_black, slots 1-5: o17 r2, o4 b4, o2 r4, o1 d4, o0 y3.
- **Bot:** will-bot69 gave a rank 2 to will-bot67, re-touching only o9. It was an
  ordinary reactive with yagami reacting: yagami holds no standing play, so this is
  not the reverse position. `reactor0.choose_very_high_clue` rung `1.reactive_play`.
- **Cause:**
  - `receiver_targets` skipped o9 as gotten, but judged the other cards on stacks
    without it: `reacter_faces` simulates the receiver's known plays only on the
    reverse arm.
  - On blue 1 the r3 and the b3 were both one away, and the leftmost won. So the
    walk read a finesse: the r3 in slot 1 through yagami's r2 in slot 1.
- **The ruling:** the target is walked on the stacks after the receiver's own known
  plays. With the b2 simulated, the b3 is a direct target, and a direct target
  comes before any finesse.
  - yagami answers from slot 3. Blue's bucket under this rank clue names green, so
    yagami reads slot 3 as the g1.
  - yagami's slot 3 is really the r4, so will-bot69's 2 was a misclue under the
    ruling.
- **Resolution (v23.29.0):**
  - On the ordinary arm, the receiver's target is now walked on the receiver's
    frame: the stacks the reacter faces, with the receiver's already gotten known
    plays laid, each as the team reads it (`gotten_plays` / `receiver_faces`,
    `src/conventions/tiiah/interpret_reactive.cpp:61-88`, `:489-518`). The
    reacter's card is still judged on the stacks it faces.
  - The receiver's reading, the giver's prediction of it, the stamp, and the
    giver's `read_clue` use the same frame.
  - The receiver plays its gotten plays before a target that sits behind them
    (`delayed_behind_own_plays`, `src/conventions/reactor0/calls.cpp:224-244`).
  - `build/replay_log.exe logs/will-bot69-2026727.log --turn 9 --rerun`: before
    `clue rank value=2 → player 1`, after `clue colour value=0 → player 0`. The 2
    is no longer given, since yagami's slot 3 is the r4.
  - On a history where yagami's slot 3 is the g1, the 2 is given, and will-bot67
    reads its called o6 as `{b3}`.
