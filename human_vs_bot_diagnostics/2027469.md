### T57 — a stall that plays nothing, spending the clue Bob needs

- **Position:** 1 clue, 2 cards left, true stacks r4 y5 g5 b3 p5 gr5
  (`scripts/tiiah_stacks.py 2027469 57`: pair and true `455355`).
  - The team needs the r5, the b4 and the b5 for 30.
  - yagami, will-bot67's Bob, holds the p2, y1, p1, r4 and a clued b5. Nothing is
    playable, and the b5 is critical.
  - will-bot69 holds the playable r5 (o47) beside its clued r1 (o37).
  - will-bot67 holds the b4 (o26) unknowingly.
- **Bot:** will-bot67 gave 1 to yagami, a trash reveal of the p1 and y1 (o43, o48).
  - Trace: `reactor0.choose_clue` rung `4.locked_eight_clues_or_low_pace`, sub-rung 4.2,
    shape `trash_reveal`, tier 0.
  - `endgame.forced_endgame_action` and `endgame.solve` both declined first.
  - Then T58 yagami discarded the p1, T59 will-bot69 played its r1 into a strike, and
    T60 will-bot67 discarded the b4.
- **Cause:** priority 4, the forced clue, gives a clue whenever Alice is locked or the
  pace is low. Nothing asks whether the token is worth more in Bob's hands. Here Bob
  held no play and a card that must not be lost, and needed a clue of his own to give.
- **Resolution (v23.35.0):** `tiiah::no_idle_stall`, a guard on the chosen action, so
  it overrides the endgame search too (tiiah/CONVENTION.md §2x).
  - Outside a forced endgame, a clue that gets no card played is replaced by the
    play/discard phase's action when Bob has no playable card and holds a critical
    card.
  - Critical here means the last usable copy: every undiscarded copy of the identity is
    in Bob's hand.
  - Rerun: `build/replay_log.exe logs/will-bot67-2027469.log --turn 57 --rerun` gives
    v23.33.0 `clue rank value=1 → player 0` (v23.31.0 the same); v23.35.0
    `discard(order=46)`, the known-trash y1.
  - Not idle stalls, so kept: a stall a forced endgame requires, a save (a clue that
    newly touches Bob's critical card, or moves a good chop or gives him a safe
    discard), and a locked Alice's clue.
  - **Test:**
    `tests/test_tiiah/test_decision_making/test_replay_2027469_no_idle_stall_bob_holds_a_critical.cpp`.
- **Status:** fixed (v23.35.0).
